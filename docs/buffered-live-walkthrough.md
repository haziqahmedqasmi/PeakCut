1. Open a creator's Setup and enable **Review buffered video for quality**.
2. Choose a 10- or 15-minute batch and describe the desired game/content focus. A reasonable starting configuration is 30–60-second clips, minimum score 90, and two selected clips per hour.
3. Choose buffer retention. Quality mode keeps at least the batch plus five minutes. Unselected footage expires; selected clips are preserved separately.
4. Configure an AI provider that accepts images. Sampled frames and speech transcripts are sent to that provider. Obtain the operator's agreement before enabling this on their recording.
5. Start recording or enable automatic recording. The live card shows the next review and recent decisions. A batch without a strong hook produces no clip.
6. For automatic publishing, connect destinations, configure exact creator credit, and enable auto-posting. Platform pauses remain independent.
7. For TikTok via Buffer, connect the TikTok channel in Buffer, enter a Buffer API key and Cloudinary credentials in Connected apps, discover the channel, and choose up to five hashtags including the required creator hashtag. Set spacing, rolling daily cap and hosting budget. Three posts/day with four-hour spacing is one conservative operator-selected configuration, not a platform guarantee.
8. Existing waiting uploads are not automatically re-reviewed. Pause or cancel that backlog separately before switching to quality-only output.
9. Verify that a real clip passes both checks, renders with credit, enters the queue once, and moves from submitted to confirmed published. Tests must use isolated media/accounts; do not publish fixtures to a real channel.
